# Public research and private evidence boundary

This public research account is based on source and results already published in this repository. Private unpublished findings, detailed local assessments, their original snapshots and private evidence belong in the separate access-controlled evidence repository. They are excluded from this documentation refresh and its archive.

[Research guide](README.md) · [Public source catalogue](source-map.md) · [Publication status](publication-status.md)

## What the public account contains

The timeline links to public source commits and experiment reports. Numerical comparisons use the existing public versioned result JSON. Historical archives preserve documentation from public Git commits, with exact-original hashes and separately rebased reading copies. Those sources retain their original qualification limits.

## What remains private

Unpublished experiment findings, vendor-specific local assessments, private decoding material, raw traces, disassembly, endpoint identifiers and supporting receipts are maintained in the appropriate private repository. A local authored report is not automatically approved for public publication merely because it is Markdown or contains aggregate numbers. The public knowledge index and archive must not copy that content or its unpublished result summaries.

## Promoting a result later

First verify the exact source/input provenance and permission to publish the selected content. Prepare a scoped public report that preserves assumptions, failures and unqualified capabilities. Review it as a new publication change. Keep the complete original private evidence and do not rewrite old measurements or imply that publication itself qualifies hardware accuracy.
